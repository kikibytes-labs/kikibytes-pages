Who we are

A small studio with a big soft spot for good ideas.

KikiBytes Labs is an independent development studio dedicated to crafting fun, professionally designed and engineered apps and games. We love experimenting, learning new things, and turning thoughtful ideas into delightful digital experiences.

We’re intentionally small, which means we can care about the details, stay close to the work, and make decisions with the people using our products in mind.

Our mission

Make useful things feel a little more human.

Our mission is to bring creativity and joy into the digital world. We strive to build experiences that are welcoming, well-made, and easy to enjoy.
