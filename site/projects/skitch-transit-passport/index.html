About the Passport

Every journey, stamped in motion.

Skitch: Transit Passport turns every public transit ride into part of a personal record you can explore, complete, and share. Track the routes you have ridden, the stations you have visited, the transfers you have made, and the transit systems that have carried you through cities around the world.

Log a journey manually, import an existing spreadsheet, or use Passport Plus to detect and save rides automatically. As your history grows, Skitch builds a visual passport filled with route coverage, system progress, maps, milestones, and stamps earned along the way.

Skitch is not another directions app. Other apps help you decide how to get somewhere. Skitch remembers how you got there. From city buses and subways to streetcars, ferries, commuter rail, and cross-country trains, every journey becomes part of the story.

Inspiration

Skitch began with a spreadsheet.

Transit enthusiasts were carefully recording the buses they had taken, train lines they had explored, stations they had visited, and systems they hoped to complete. The information was meaningful, but it was trapped inside rows, columns, and formulas.

We saw something bigger than a log; we saw a personal travel history. From the routes that shaped a daily routine, to the systems discovered in new cities, and the journeys remembered long after reaching the destination. That idea became a more visual, personal, and rewarding way to collect a lifetime of transit experiences.
