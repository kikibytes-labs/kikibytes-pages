Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 12, 2026

KikiBytes LLC ("KikiBytes," "we," "us," or "our") operates the public KikiBytes Labs website at kikibytes.com and the KikiBytes pages or services that expressly link to this Privacy Policy. This policy explains what information we collect, where it comes from, why we use it, when we disclose it, and the choices that may be available to you.

This policy does not automatically apply to a future KikiBytes app or game that has its own privacy notice. If a service-specific notice conflicts with this policy for that service, the service-specific notice controls.

At a glance

  • We collect contact details and messages you choose to send, limited website delivery and security data, and an on-device contact-form cooldown timestamp.
  • We use Cloudflare for hosting, security, and privacy-focused website analytics, and FormSubmit to process contact-form submissions.
  • We do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it to build advertising profiles.
  • Future apps and games will need service-specific privacy review before they collect information not described here.

Information we collect and where it comes from

Information you provide. If you use the contact form or email us, we receive information directly from you, such as your name, email address, subject, message, and any other information you choose to include. Providing this information is voluntary, but we may be unable to respond without a working email address and enough information to understand your request. Please do not send passwords, payment-card numbers, government identifiers, health information, or other sensitive information.

Website delivery and security data. Cloudflare and other systems used to deliver and secure the website may process technical request data such as an IP address, date and time, requested page, referring page, browser or device information, and security events. We use this information only for website delivery, reliability, security, troubleshooting, and legal compliance—not to create advertising profiles.

Website analytics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand website performance and usage. Its beacon records page views, page-load performance, and related dimensions such as page path, referrer, country, device type, browser, operating system, and navigation type. Cloudflare states that Web Analytics does not collect or use visitors’ personal data and does not track individual visitors across its customers’ websites.

Form-processing data. When you submit the contact form, FormSubmit receives the visible form fields, the form’s anti-spam and delivery fields, and ordinary connection and security data generated by the request, such as an IP address, browser information, time, and referring origin. If JavaScript is unavailable, FormSubmit may display its hosted response page and anti-spam tools, including Google reCAPTCHA.

Information stored on your device. After a successful contact-form submission, our JavaScript stores a timestamp in your browser’s local storage to provide a one-minute browser-side cooldown. The timestamp is not included with the form submission and remains on your device until it is replaced or you clear your browser storage.

Cookies, analytics, and browser privacy signals

We do not use cookies for advertising or cross-site behavioral analytics. Cloudflare may use strictly necessary technologies in limited circumstances to deliver or protect the website. FormSubmit and its anti-spam provider may use their own technologies when your browser communicates with their services. Those providers’ practices are governed by their own notices.

We do not permit third parties to collect personal information through this website over time and across unrelated websites for behavioral advertising. Because the website does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising and does not engage in that form of cross-site tracking, Global Privacy Control and “Do Not Track” signals do not change how the website currently operates. If our practices change, we will update this policy and honor legally required browser signals.

How we use information and our legal bases

We use information to operate, deliver, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the website; respond to messages and project inquiries; prevent spam and misuse; maintain appropriate business records; comply with legal obligations; and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Where a law requires us to identify a legal basis, we rely on our legitimate interests in operating and securing the website and communicating with people who contact us; steps taken at your request before entering into a contract; compliance with legal obligations; and consent when we specifically request it. We do not use contact or analytics information for automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

How we disclose information

We disclose information only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Hosting, security, and analytics providers. Cloudflare delivers and protects the website and provides Web Analytics.
  • Form and communications providers. FormSubmit processes contact-form submissions and delivers them to hello@kikibytes.com; our email provider then processes the resulting correspondence. FormSubmit’s privacy terms govern its processing.
  • Professional advisers and authorities. We may disclose information to advisers, insurers, courts, regulators, or law enforcement when reasonably necessary to comply with law or protect rights, safety, and the integrity of our services.
  • Business transactions. Information may be reviewed or transferred in connection with a financing, merger, acquisition, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of all or part of our business, subject to appropriate protections.

We do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or authorize service providers to use contact submissions for their own advertising.

Retention and deletion

We retain contact messages and related correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain appropriate business and security records, resolve disputes, comply with law, and enforce agreements. FormSubmit states that it retains submissions for 30 days. Cloudflare states that it retains unsampled Web Analytics data for seven days and makes aggregated dashboard metrics available for six months. Cloudflare, FormSubmit, and our email provider otherwise control their own service logs and retention under their terms and settings.

We may retain a record longer when a legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, security, or claims-related need requires it. When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or deidentify it, subject to backup cycles and legal obligations. The local cooldown timestamp remains on your device until it is replaced or cleared.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information we handle and the risks involved, including limiting the information requested by the contact form and using encrypted network connections. No internet transmission or storage system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your privacy rights and choices

Depending on where you live, whether an applicable law covers KikiBytes, and the nature of our relationship, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a portable copy of your personal information; object to or restrict certain processing; withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; opt out of covered sale, sharing, or targeted advertising; appeal a decision about a request; and complain to a regulator.

To make a request, email hello@kikibytes.com with the subject “Privacy request” and describe the request and the jurisdiction where you live. We may need to verify your identity and authority before acting. An authorized agent may submit a request where applicable law permits. If applicable law gives you an appeal right, reply to our decision with the subject “Privacy appeal.” We will not discriminate against you for exercising an applicable privacy right.

California notice. California’s Consumer Privacy Act applies only when its statutory coverage requirements are met. If it applies to KikiBytes and your information, the sections above describe the categories we collect, sources, purposes, retention criteria, and categories of recipients. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by that law, and we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes that require a right to limit.

Children’s privacy

The website is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. Children under 13 should not use the contact form or send us personal information. If we learn that a submission came from a child under 13 without authorization, we will promptly delete it or, where legally permitted and reasonably necessary, use the contact address for a single response and then promptly delete it from systems we control and ask applicable providers to do the same. A parent or guardian who believes a child contacted us should email us with the subject “Child privacy.”

Before a future KikiBytes app or game collects information from children or is directed to a younger audience, it will require a separate age-design and children’s privacy review and, when appropriate, a service-specific notice and parental-consent process.

International visitors

KikiBytes is based in New York, United States. If you use the website from another country, information you submit may be processed in the United States and in other countries where our service providers operate. Privacy laws in those countries may differ from those where you live. Depending on where you live and whether a law covers KikiBytes, that law may provide additional rights or safeguards.

Third-party links

The website links to third-party websites and services, including social-media platforms. Those links are not embedded social plug-ins, and those third parties do not receive information from KikiBytes merely because the links appear here. If you follow a link, the third party’s own terms and privacy practices apply.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services, providers, or legal obligations change. We will post the revised policy here, update the effective date, and provide a prominent website notice or direct notice when required for a material change. The revised policy applies from its stated effective date.

Contact

KikiBytes LLC is the business responsible for the information practices described here. Privacy questions, requests, and concerns can be sent to hello@kikibytes.com. KikiBytes LLC is based in New York, New York, United States.
