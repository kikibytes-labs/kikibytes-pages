Privacy policy

KikiBytes LLC ("KikiBytes," "we," "us," or "our") operates the KikiBytes Labs website and related pages that link to this policy. This policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect information in connection with those services.

Information we collect

Information you provide. If you contact us, we receive the information you submit, such as your name, email address, subject, and message. Please do not send passwords, payment-card numbers, health information, or other sensitive information through the contact form.

Information collected automatically. Our hosting and security systems may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the website, such as an IP address, browser or device information, referring page, requested page, and date and time of a request. We do not use this information to create advertising profiles.

Local storage. Our contact form may store a timestamp in your browser’s local storage to help limit repeated submissions. This value is stored on your device and is not submitted to us as part of the form.

Analytics and cookies

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregated website performance and usage. Cloudflare states that its Web Analytics product does not collect or use visitors’ personal data. Our website does not use advertising cookies or third-party behavioral advertising. The third-party services used to process a form submission may have their own cookies or technologies when you interact with them; their practices are governed by their own policies.

How we use information

We use information to provide and secure the website, respond to questions and feedback, operate and improve our services, prevent abuse, comply with legal obligations, and establish or defend legal claims. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

How we share information

We may disclose information to service providers that help us operate the website. Contact-form submissions are sent through FormSubmit and delivered to hello@kikibytes.com. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets. We do not authorize service providers to use submitted information for their own advertising.

Retention and deletion

We retain contact messages and related correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain business records, resolve disputes, comply with law, and enforce agreements. FormSubmit says it retains form submissions in its archive for 30 days. We may retain an email or business record longer when there is a legitimate legal or operational need. You may request deletion at any time, subject to information we must retain by law or for legitimate claims.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information and the risks of processing it. No internet transmission or storage system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live and the law that applies, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of your personal information; to object to or restrict certain processing; to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and to complain to a data-protection authority. California residents may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended, including rights to know, delete, correct, opt out of sale or sharing, limit certain uses of sensitive personal information, and receive equal treatment. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are used in that law.

To make a request, email hello@kikibytes.com with the subject “Privacy request.” We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. You may use an authorized agent where applicable law permits. We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable rights.

Children’s privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can investigate and delete it when appropriate. Any future app or game may have additional age and children’s privacy terms before launch.

KikiBytes is based in New York, United States. If you access the website from another country, your information may be processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate. Where required, we use an appropriate legal mechanism for cross-border transfers.

Third-party links

The website may link to third-party websites and services, including social-media platforms and project sites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Review their policies before providing information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services or legal obligations change. We will post the updated policy here and change the “Last updated” date. The revised policy applies from its effective date.
